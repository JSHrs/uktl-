Questions clients ask

Before you brief us.

How is Talent Compass different from an ATS filter?

An ATS filters out. Compass ranks every candidate against weightings you set and shows the evidence for each score. A consultant reviews the top of the list before anything reaches you.

Do you only recruit for GCC roles?

No. We recruit in the UK and across the Gulf, and many searches cover both, for example UK-trained professionals moving to Riyadh or Dubai.

How confidential is a search?

Approaches are made without naming you until a candidate is interested and has agreed to confidentiality. Your portal is visible only to the people you invite.

Can we use the portal without a retained search?

Yes. You can post roles and see ranked applicants on a per-role basis. Talk to us about pricing.
