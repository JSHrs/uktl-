UK Talent Link uses the information you provide to operate your account, assess your CV, suggest relevant vacancies and respond to workplace questions or consultation requests.

What the platform processes

Account contact details, uploaded CVs and extracted career information, assessment results, job interests, workplace questions, consultation records and technical security records. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in your CV or questions.

For employer clients, we process contact details for your team, the roles you post, and your notes and decisions on candidates.

AI and service providers

CV assessment and some guidance use Anthropic’s AI service. The platform also uses infrastructure, private file storage, vacancy, booking and email services. AI assessments may be incomplete or incorrect. You can correct your extracted profile and ask the team to review a result; a score is not an employment decision.

Access and your requests

Candidate records are restricted to the account owner and authorised staff. From your profile you can download a copy of your data, request a full reviewed export or ask for erasure. The team reviews each request before confirming completion.

Cookies and retention

Sign-in uses session cookies to keep your account secure. Contact the team for the retention period that applies to your records, or to raise a privacy concern.
